The Community

Independent professionals. A shared community.

Summit Club 64 brings together independent sourcing and recruiting contractors working with technology companies. The purpose is simple: help one another, strengthen each member’s independent practice, and better serve the professional talent networks behind the work.

Sourcing & Recruiting

A community for individual practitioners, including contract and embedded recruiting professionals.

Technology & Knowledge

Practical tools, AI-enabled sourcing methods, talent intelligence, and peer learning.

Trusted Networks

Relationships built on relevance, discretion, and professional judgment—not outreach volume.
