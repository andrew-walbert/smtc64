Privacy

Membership application privacy notice.

This notice covers information submitted through the Summit Club 64 membership application.

Information submitted

The application asks for your name, email address, the referring member's name, your current role and organization, and what you are working toward in your career. Please do not submit passwords, government identification numbers, confidential employer information, or other sensitive personal information.

How it is used

Summit Club 64 uses application responses to review membership fit and communicate with applicants about their application. Responses are submitted through Google Forms. Google's processing of information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Questions about an application

For questions about information you submitted, contact aew@smtc64.com.
